About HFCB Group

HF Group Limited is a Kenya-based mortgage finance institution. The Company operates in two segments: Mortgages and Deposits mobilization. The Mortgages segment covers Retail mortgages, which is responsible for sourcing residential mortgages for individual owner occupiers; Schemes mortgages division is responsible for arranging corporate mortgage packages with employers; Projects division provides lending to property developers for construction, including construction of residential houses, office blocks, schools, hospitals and other related infrastructure; Retail deposits plays a critical role in the operations of the Company sourcing for deposits from retail customers which are then used to finance the Company's mortgage products and Corporate deposits is responsible for sourcing for deposits from corporate organizations. The Deposit mobilization segment includes two divisions, namely Retail deposits and Corporate deposits.
