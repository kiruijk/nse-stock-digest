About Deacons East Africa

Deacons (East Africa) Plc (previously Deacons Kenya Limited) was incorporated in Kenya, as a private limited liability company under the Companies Act on March 20, 1973. It was subsequently converted to a public company on October 26. Before then, Deacons operated as a Marks & Spencer franchisee since 1958. Deacons's principal business is to operate retail establishments including franchise and department stores selling ladies, men's and children's clothing, footwear, accessories, toiletries, gifts, home-furnishings, cosmetics and sporting goods among other items in East Africa.NOTE: Shares of Deacons East Africa are currently suspended from trading on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE) since close of trading on Friday, November 16, 2018.
