About Homeboyz Entertainment

Homeboyz Entertainment Limited is an entertainment company offering bespoke services in event production. Established in 1992 as a small DJ agency catering for weddings and house parties, the company has since grown to become the biggest and most influential entertainment company in the region. Homeboyz is listed as one of the top 10 event production companies in Africa and has attracted a wide spectrum of clients locally and around the region, with a vision to provide unique, no-compromise approach to event production. Homeboyz Entertainment’s clients include Warner Bros, Disney, Rockefeller Foundation, United Nations, Microsoft, Google, BBC, Tiger Aspect (UK), Endemol, American Embassy, British Embassy, Samsung, and LG.
