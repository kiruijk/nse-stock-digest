About Bamburi Cement Ltd

Bamburi Cement Limited is a Kenya-based company engaged in the manufacture and sale of cement and cement-related products. It also owns and manages a nature and environmental park developed from rehabilitated quarries. The Company's subsidiaries include Bamburi Special Products Ltd, which is engaged in manufacturing concrete paving blocks and other cement products under the brand name BamburiBlox, and Lafarge Eco Systems Ltd, which manages the Company's mining reserve lands and rehabilitates its quarries. Its other subsidiaries include Bamburi Cement Ltd, Uganda, HimCem Holdings Limited, Diani Estate Limited, Kenya Cement Marketing Ltd, Portland Mines Limited and Seruji Management Limited.
